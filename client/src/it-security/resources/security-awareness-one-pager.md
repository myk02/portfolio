# Stay Safe Online — one-pager for staff (Template)

> **Template** — prepared by Mike Waitindi for non-technical staff, not a record of work done for an employer.

## Phishing (fake messages)

- Stop, look, think: unexpected message asking you to click, pay, or "verify"? Pause.
- Check the sender address carefully, not just the name.
- Do not open attachments or links you did not expect. Ask IT first — no one will blame you for checking.
- If you clicked: disconnect, tell IT immediately, do not hide it. Fast reporting limits damage.

## Passwords

- Use a long password you can remember (four random words work well).
- Never reuse your work password anywhere else.
- Turn on the second step (code on your phone) wherever it is offered.
- Never share passwords by email or chat. If someone needs access, IT grants it properly.

## Safe data handling

- Share sensitive files only with people who need them, inside approved systems.
- Lock your screen whenever you stand up (Windows key + L / Ctrl+Cmd+Q).
- Report a lost device or strange account activity to IT the same day.
- When in doubt, ask. A two-minute question beats a week-long incident.
